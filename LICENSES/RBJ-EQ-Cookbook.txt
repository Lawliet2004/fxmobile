Robert Bristow-Johnson Audio EQ Cookbook
========================================

Filter coefficient formulas used by FxMobile's biquad implementation are
derived from "Cookbook formulae for audio equalizer biquad filter
coefficients" by Robert Bristow-Johnson.

The cookbook is widely treated as freely reusable public-domain DSP
reference material. No cookbook source files are vendored in this tree;
only the published mathematical formulas were reimplemented.
